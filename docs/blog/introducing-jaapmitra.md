# Introducing JaapMitra: A Peaceful Digital Mala for Your Daily Naam Jap

*Bhakti ka saathi, har din.*

For centuries, the mala has been a quiet companion: 108 beads, one mantra, one
breath at a time. But most of us now carry our day in our pocket, and the
mala often gets left behind. JaapMitra ("friend of jap") is built for that
moment: a calm, private digital mala that is ready the second you open it.

## Why we built it

Most jap counters we tried felt like apps first and prayer second: ads between
beads, sign-ups before the first count, small buttons that need your eyes.
We wanted the opposite. Open the app, and you are counting within a second.

## What makes it different

**The whole screen is the button.** Tap anywhere to count a bead. No aiming,
no looking. A gentle haptic marks every bead and a stronger one marks every
completed mala, so you can chant with your eyes closed and still know where
you are.

**A real mala.** A 108-bead mala fills as you chant. Prefer 54 or 27? Set any
mala size you like. Made a mistake? Undo it, add counts by hand, or reset the
current mala.

**Chant in the dark.** Blackout mode gives you a pure black screen with just
two buttons, so you can chant at night without lighting up the room.
Meditation mode adds a session timer and calming music, either the built-in
loops or your own recording.

**Your mantra, your script.** 21 sacred mantras come built in, in Devanagari:
Ram, Radha, Om Namah Shivaya, Hare Krishna, Gayatri, Mahamrityunjaya,
Waheguru and more. Add your own in any script, each with its own mala size.

**Sadhana, not just a number.** Set a daily goal, take a Sankalp (a vow of
11, 21, 40 days or more), and watch each day get ticked off. Streaks,
charts, a habit grid and lifetime milestones, from your first 1,008 Jaap to
a crore, show how steady your practice has become.

**Made for India's languages.** English, हिन्दी, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ,
தமிழ் and తెలుగు, including the reminder notifications.

## Private by design

Your devotion is nobody's business. JaapMitra has no account, no ads, no
analytics and no network calls. Everything you chant stays on your device and
works fully offline. You can export a readable backup and restore it whenever
you like.

## Also in the app

- Seven short devotional stories in English and Hindi, with read-aloud
- Reminders for your Jaap, your streak, and Ekadashi and festival mornings
- A home screen widget, and a lock-screen +1 button on iOS 17+
- Counting with volume buttons, a headset button or a Bluetooth clicker
- 13 colour themes, and your own photo behind the counter
