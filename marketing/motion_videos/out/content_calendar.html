Guardrails

  • Manifestation posts are about intention and focus. Never promise money, health, love or other outcomes.
  • Say “traditionally” or “one reading”. Practices differ by sampradaya.
  • UGC results must be the creator’s own experience. No medical or mental-health claims.
  • Sikh posts use the chimes palette (no bells or conch). Have a Sikh reviewer check the copy.
  • Children on camera: no names, schools or locations.
  • Follow temple photography rules and ask before filming people.
  • Gita Jayanti is listed as 19 or 20 Dec. Confirm with a Panchang before scheduling.
  • Have a native Hindi speaker read every Hindi post before it goes out.
